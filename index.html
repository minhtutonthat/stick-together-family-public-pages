Everything your family needs, in one place

Stick Together Family keeps every member organised, connected, and on the same page — from the youngest child to the parents running the household.

✅

Tasks & Chores

Create task lists, assign chores to family members, set due dates, reminders, and track completion together.

📅

Family Calendar

Shared calendar with events, time-blocked activities, and automatic reminders synced across every device.

🛒

Shopping Lists

Collaborative grocery lists and project shopping — add items from anywhere, check them off in the store.

🍳

Family Recipes

Build a private cookbook with your favourite family recipes, complete with ingredients and step-by-step instructions.

📖

Family Diary

Capture memories with photos, captions, and comments shared only within your family.

📋

Task Templates

Built-in templates for holidays, school mornings, cleaning days, and more. Customise or create your own.

Routines

Daily and weekly habit checklists for every family member, with step-by-step guidance and progress tracking.

Designed for every family member

Each member has a role with the right level of access — parents stay in control while kids and teens stay engaged.

👑

Admin

Full access. Manages family settings, roles, Google Drive setup, and subscriptions.

👨‍👩‍

Parent

Full household access including finances, tasks, shopping, calendar, and diary.

🧑

Teen

Access to all shared content except finance. Ideal for older children helping at home.

🧒

Child

Sees only their assigned tasks, routines, music, and the virtual pet. Safe and focused.

Simple, family-wide subscription

One subscription covers every member of your family — no per-seat pricing.

  • Free trial: the first 30 days after creating a family are completely free.
  • Family plan: a single subscription unlocks the full app for all members.
  • Subscriptions are managed securely by Google Play — we never handle payment details.
  • Cancel any time directly from your Play Store account.

Google Drive integration

Attach photos and files to tasks and diary entries. Upload family songs. Everything is stored in a dedicated "Stick Together Family" folder in your own Google Drive — you always own your data. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.
